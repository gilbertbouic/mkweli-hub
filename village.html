Integrity Village

A short practice in public contracting and unexplained wealth. Adults only. The grid is the exercise. This page is the cover.

Who it is for

Adults who already touch money or public claims: programme finance, procurement, municipal works, cooperative treasurers, NGO logistics, compliance, editors.

Not a children’s activity, and not entertainment.

What you practise

  1. Read a works specification and choose the disclosed, lawful bid. Familiarity with a contractor is not a reason to award the house.
  2. File a brief on unexplained wealth that a handler could use: an observable fact, a date, a document or a named gap. Not an accusation, and not gossip joined to a name.

What it is not

Fiction. A training node set in Terre Rouge. Not a court. Not a filing channel. Not a report about a named living person. Simulated stakes are there so a weak choice has a consequence. They are not instructions.

How long

Round 1 is eight contracts: the pump, two blocks, the school, the substation, drainage, street lighting, and the community centre. A clean sweep unlocks round 2, five briefs. Reset is for a second pass or a group of six to twelve. Allow about forty-five minutes with a debrief.

How Grok is used

Grok is used to draft and challenge the exercise. It is not a source, and it does not choose the bid. The learner decides.

Debrief · Round 1

Contracting. Read this after the eight bids, not before.

  • Only the disclosed, lawful option scores in full. A wrong answer scores less. The points show what you logged. They are not a judgment.
  • A correct top-score bid awards the renovation. A wrong bid, or a second-place bid, is rejected. The building stays as it is.
  • The four contractors are labels in a drill. Do not treat a name as a finding about a real firm.

In a workplace: write the specification you used, the option you chose, and why the others fail the disclosed requirement. Then stop. Do not award the work because the bidder is known.

Debrief · Round 2

Unexplained wealth. This round stays locked until round 1 is a clean sweep.

  • A usable brief can move a case forward. A thin brief is rejected. A reckless brief turns on the person who filed it. Those are simulated outcomes.
  • A handler can use: an observable fact, a date, a document, or a document that is missing. A handler cannot use a rumour with a name attached.
  • Do not join a public identity to a private suspicion on this page. The exercise keeps those registers apart on purpose.

In a workplace: if a payment should not go out, use the sanctions check. If a public figure is missing, leave the gap marked unpublished. Escalate to a named handler. This site is not that handler.
