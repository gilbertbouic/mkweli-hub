FAQ

What the price does and does not include.

Who owns the website?

You do. Files are yours. The domain is registered in your name with the registrar you pay. Monthly care is a retainer for edits, not a lock on the site.

Is the domain included?

No. You buy it; we connect it (GitHub Pages DNS). A typical .com is much cheaper than .mu. We will say so before you pay anyone. A Vitrine can go live on a mkweli.tech address until you have a name. Renewal is your registrar bill, not the Rs 500 care fee.

What is monthly care?

Site includes 12 months of 1 small edit per month (text, photo, hours, or swap the video URL). After month 12: optional Rs 500/month for the same cap. Vitrine: Rs 500/month optional, not required. If you stop, the site stays up; the edit hours stop. We do not hold the domain hostage.

What does Rs 5,000 include?

One page: façade, hours, WhatsApp, map, English and French. Domain not included. It is not a 5-page site and not a web shop.

What does Rs 15,000 include?

Up to 5 pages, English and French, contact/WhatsApp, basic SEO, Google Business link, one revision round, one embed of a video you already have. Year 1: one small edit per month. You supply photos, facts, and the video URL. Domain not included. No e-commerce, booking, login, extra pages, or filming. Those are Custom.

Do you work outside Grand Baie?

Yes. The studio is in Grand Baie. Websites are for businesses anywhere in Mauritius. Custom work also includes clients abroad (for example XL Highway Travel in Durban).

English and French, not Creole?

Client pages are English and French. WhatsApp with us can be whichever language you use.
