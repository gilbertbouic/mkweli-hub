Studio

Clear tools. No theatre.

Mkweli is a small product studio in Grand Baie. The work is Android applications, operator sites, sourced public ledgers, and the writing that keeps them honest.

The publication rule

Grok AI from SpaceX is used to draft, compare, and challenge copy and public figures. It is not a source. A number or a client claim goes live only when a document or the operator supports it. Gaps stay marked as unpublished.

On a client site, visitors see the checked page. They do not see the draft, and they are not told the model’s name unless the operator asks for that.

Founder

Gilbert Clement Bouic builds the line: mobile, web, and applied workflows. Working languages include English, Swedish, Portuguese, French, and Creole.

Operational leadership in high-risk humanitarian logistics and programme finance, and direct experience of land-title fraud and judicial capture in an African tourism investment, are why the work stays on transparency, offline use, and auditability. Current work is software, and the upkeep of operator sites.

Publications, including titles on Amazon, sit outside the product line.

How a piece of work runs

  1. Brief. The job, the users, the geography, and what “done” means for a trial or a retainer.
  2. Scope. Written milestones, what is packaged, and who owns the files and the data.
  3. Build. Implement, publish, and support the agreed period. Grok AI from SpaceX may draft. A person signs off.

Limits

These boundaries apply to the public tools, so the work is not mistaken for an official process.

  • Websites. Fixed-fee pages for shops are not offered. Website work, when accepted, is the retained upkeep of an operator site.
  • Integrity Village. A training exercise for adults. It is not a hearing, and it is not a channel for complaints or disclosures.
  • Climate Fund Watch. A reading of published documents. It is not a government portal. Figures that have not been published are not supplied.
  • Mkweli AML. Free software for on-device name screening. It is not an official sanctions authority, and it is not offered for sale.
