Studio

Clear tools. Checked pages.

Mkweli is a small studio in Grand Baie. It maintains sites, builds tools, runs training sessions, and takes product roles.

The publication rule

AI drafts and checks the wording. A figure or a client claim is published only when a document or the operator confirms it. If the source is missing, the gap stays visible.

On a client site, visitors see the finished page in the operator’s own words.

Founder

Gilbert Clement Bouic builds the line: mobile, web, and applied workflows. Working languages include English, Swedish, Portuguese, French, and Creole.

The focus comes from operational leadership in humanitarian logistics and programme finance, and from direct experience of land-title fraud and judicial capture in an African tourism investment. The current work is software, and keeping live sites accurate. That is what clients hire.

Publications, including titles on Amazon, sit outside the product line.

How a piece of work runs

  1. Brief. The job, the users, the place, and what finished means.
  2. Scope. Written milestones, what is packaged, and who owns the files and the data.
  3. Build. Build, publish, and support the agreed period. AI may draft. A person signs off.
