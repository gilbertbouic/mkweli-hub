Read-along stories · ages 6–10

Children's Stories

Picture stories about children across Africa who face drought, storms, rising seas and disappearing wildlife, and find brave, clever ways to help. Written by Sinjonjo.

Each story is read aloud, with every word lit up as it is spoken. Tap the underlined words to learn what they mean, and finish with a “What would you do?” question.

The books are available on Barnes & Noble and Amazon, listed under the author's own name, Gilbert Bouic.

Four books · twelve stories

Choose a book

Book 1 · water

Combatting Water Scarcity Across Africa

Three picture stories about children who find clever ways to bring water to their villages.

Kwame and the Sand-Dam · Kaya & Rain-Collection · Zara and the Aqua-Cleaner

Book 2 · extreme weather

Fighting Extreme Weather Across Africa

Three picture stories about brave children facing sandstorms, floods and cyclones.

Adila and the Sirocco's Embrace · Bikyezi and Kagote against the Tropical Deluge · Juma and the Cyclone

Book 3 · rising seas

Rising Sea Levels Across Africa

Three picture stories about children who help protect their seaside homes from the rising sea.

Lila and the Mermaids Fight Rising Seas · Amani and Nia Defend Africa's Shores · Ayana and the Rescue of Seashell Bay

Book 4 · wildlife and nature

Champions of Africa's Biodiversity Struggle

Three picture stories about children who help bees, birds and elephants, and bring nature back to life.

Zara's Quest for the Last Golden Bee · Malik and the Rainbow Songbird · Amina and the Elephant Oracle

Read-along picture stories for children aged 6–10, written by Sinjonjo. A Mkweli project from Grand Baie, Mauritius.
