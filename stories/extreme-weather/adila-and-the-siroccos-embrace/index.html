Words to learn

Dunes
Hills of sand made by the wind.
Extreme weather
Weather that is much wilder than usual, like huge storms, floods or heatwaves.
Flock
A group of animals, like sheep or goats, that stay together.
Horizon
The line far away where the land or sea seems to meet the sky.
Legend
A famous story that people tell for many, many years.
Nomads
People who move from place to place with their animals instead of living in one village.
Oasis
A green place in the desert where there is water, so plants can grow.
Sahara
The biggest hot desert in the world, in the north of Africa.
Sirocco
A hot, dusty wind that blows from the Sahara desert. It can whip up huge sandstorms.
Tribes
Groups of families who share the same language, customs and history.

What would you do?

Adila knew exactly what to do when the sirocco came. If you saw a big sandstorm coming, what would you do?

  • 🏠 Go inside a strong shelter and close the doors and windows Yes! A strong shelter keeps the sand and wind out. Stay inside until the storm has passed.
  • 🧣 Cover my nose, mouth and eyes with a scarf Smart! A scarf over your face stops the sand from getting into your nose, mouth and eyes, just like Adila's headscarf.
  • 🐐 Bring the animals to safety and keep water ready Just like Adila guiding her flock! Animals need shelter and water too.
  • 👂 Listen to the elders and the weather warnings Very wise! Adila learned from the elders. Warnings help us get ready before the storm arrives.

Talk about it with a grown-up or your class. There is more than one good answer!
