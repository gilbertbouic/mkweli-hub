Words to learn

Ancestors
Family members who lived long, long ago, like your great-great-grandparents.
Bamboo
A very tall, strong grass with hollow stems. People build with it.
Batwa
A people who have lived in the forests of central Africa for thousands of years.
Canopy
The very top of the forest, where the tree branches and leaves meet like a roof.
Channels
Little ditches dug in the ground so water can flow away safely.
Congo Basin
A huge area of rainforest in the middle of Africa, around the Congo River.
Deluge
A huge amount of rain that falls very quickly and can cause floods.
Orchestra
A big group of musicians playing together.
Rainforest
A thick, green forest where it rains almost every day.
Silverback
A grown-up male gorilla. The hair on his back turns silver as he gets older.

What would you do?

Bikyezi and Kagote helped the water flow away and kept everyone safe. If heavy rain started flooding your home, what would you do?

  • ⛰️ Go to higher ground with my family Yes! Water flows downhill, so higher ground is safer, just like Mubaku leading his family uphill.
  • 🚫 Never walk, swim or play in flood water Very wise! Flood water can be deep, fast and dirty, even when it looks calm.
  • ⛏️ Help grown-ups keep drains and channels clear Just like Bikyezi and Kagote! Clear channels guide the water away from homes.
  • 🌳 Plant trees and protect the forest Great idea! Trees and roots soak up rain and hold the soil, so floods are smaller.

Talk about it with a grown-up or your class. There is more than one good answer!
