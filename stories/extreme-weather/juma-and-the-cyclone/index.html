Words to learn

Archipelago
A group of islands close together.
Cyclone
A giant spinning storm with very strong winds and heavy rain. It starts over warm sea water.
Drum
A musical instrument you beat with your hands or sticks. Drums are used to share music and messages.
Elder
An older, wise person who is respected in the community.
Flood
When lots of water covers land that is usually dry.
Mangroves
Trees that grow in salty water along the coast. Their tangled roots protect the land from big waves.
Mwani
A people who live on the coast and islands of northern Mozambique.
Quirimbas
A group of islands off the coast of Mozambique, in East Africa.
Rhythm
The pattern of beats in music.
Traditions
Ways of doing things that families pass on from grandparents to grandchildren.

What would you do?

Remizio's mud kept the roofs on, and Juma's music kept spirits high. If a cyclone was coming to your island, what would you do?

  • 🏚️ Help make the roof strong and tie down loose things Just like Remizio's secret! Strong roofs and tied-down things stay put in strong winds.
  • 🎒 Pack an emergency bag with water, food and a torch Well prepared! An emergency bag helps your family if the power goes off or you need to leave.
  • 🪟 Stay inside, away from windows, until it is safe Very wise! Flying things can break windows. Wait until grown-ups say the storm is over.
  • 🌿 Plant and protect mangroves along the shore Brilliant! Mangrove roots slow down big waves and protect the coast from cyclones.

Talk about it with a grown-up or your class. There is more than one good answer!
