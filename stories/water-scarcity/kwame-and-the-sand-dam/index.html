Words to learn

Baobab
A giant African tree with a very thick trunk. It can store water inside it!
Rainwater harvesting
Catching the rain (for example from roofs) and keeping it in tanks or ponds to use later.
Reservoir
A place where lots of water is stored to use later.
Riverbed
The bottom of a river. In the dry season it can be just sand.
Sand-dam
A wall built across a sandy riverbed. When the river flows, sand piles up behind the wall and water stays hidden under the sand, ready to use when it is dry.
Savanna
Wide, grassy land with a few trees. Much of Africa is savanna.

What would you do?

Many places in Africa don't get enough water. But when it rains, we can catch and keep the rainwater! If you lived in Kwame's village, how would you save water?

  • 🏠 Put a tank under the roof to catch the rain Great idea! Rain from the roof can run down a pipe into a big tank. That is called rainwater harvesting.
  • 🪣 Build a sand-dam across the river with friends Just like Kwame! A sand-dam keeps water hidden safely under the sand for the dry season.
  • 🚰 Use less water and never waste a drop Every drop counts! Turning off the tap and reusing water helps everyone.
  • 🌱 Plant trees so the ground keeps the rain Wonderful! Trees and plants help the ground soak up rain and keep it cool.

Talk about it with a grown-up or your class. There is more than one good answer!
