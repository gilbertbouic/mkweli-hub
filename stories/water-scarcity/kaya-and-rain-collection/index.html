Words to learn

Basins
Big, wide bowls for holding water.
Climate
The usual weather of a place over many years. When the climate changes, rain can come less often.
Drought
A long time with little or no rain, when rivers and wells dry up.
Grove
A small group of trees growing together.
Necessity is the mother of invention
When we really need something, we think up clever new ideas to get it!
Quest
A long journey to find something important.
Rainwater
Rain that we catch, in barrels, basins or tanks, so we can use it later.
Savanna
Wide, grassy land with a few trees, where it is warm all year. Much of Africa is savanna.
Well
A deep hole dug into the ground to reach water hidden underneath. People lower a bucket to bring the water up.
Well-bucket
The bucket people lower on a rope into a well to pull up water.

What would you do?

Kaya's village learned to save the rain. Necessity is the mother of invention: when we really need something, we think up clever new ideas! If the well in your village ran dry, what would you do?

  • 🛢️ Put barrels and basins out to collect the rain Just like Kaya's village! Every rain shower can fill your basins for the dry days.
  • 🥬 Grow a garden that needs only a little water Smart! Plants that need little water, and mulch to keep the soil damp, save lots of water.
  • 🧺 Cover water containers so it doesn't dry up Good thinking! A lid stops the sun from drying up the water and keeps it clean.
  • 💡 Invent something new to catch more rain That's the spirit! When we need water, we invent new ways to get it: maybe a gutter, a funnel, or something nobody has thought of yet!

Talk about it with a grown-up or your class. There is more than one good answer!
