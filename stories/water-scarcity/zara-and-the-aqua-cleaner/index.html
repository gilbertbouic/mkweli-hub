Words to learn

Aqua-Cleaner
Zara's invention! A machine that takes in dirty river water and cleans it so it is safe.
Filter
Something that lets water pass through but catches dirt. Layers of sand, gravel and cloth can make a simple filter.
Gadgets
Small, clever machines or tools.
Invent
To make something new that nobody has made before.
Inventor
A person who makes something new that nobody has made before.
Legend
A famous story that people tell for many, many years.
Murky
Dark and dirty, so you can't see through it.
Solar-powered
Working on energy from the sun, using solar panels.
Workshop
A room where people make and fix things.

What would you do?

Zara turned a murky river into clean water. If the river near your home looked dark and smelly, what would you do?

  • 🫗 Never drink it until it has been cleaned and boiled Very wise! Boiling water kills germs. Always ask a grown-up first.
  • 🧪 Make a simple filter with sand, gravel and cloth Clever inventor! A filter catches the dirt. Then a grown-up should boil the water before anyone drinks it.
  • 🚯 Keep rubbish out of the river and tell a grown-up Great! Keeping rivers clean stops the water from getting dirty in the first place.
  • ☀️ Invent a solar-powered gadget, like Zara Amazing! Young inventors all over Africa are building gadgets to clean water.

Talk about it with a grown-up or your class. There is more than one good answer!
