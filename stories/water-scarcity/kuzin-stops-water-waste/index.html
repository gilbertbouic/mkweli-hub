Words to learn

Corrugated
Wavy, like the metal roofs on many Mauritian houses.
CWA
The Central Water Authority, which brings tap water to homes in Mauritius. Its free number is 170.
Detective
Someone who looks carefully for clues to solve a mystery.
Dodo
A bird that lived only in Mauritius and disappeared long ago.
Drought
A long time with very little rain.
Float (in a tank)
A part that floats on the water and turns the water off when the tank is full.
Granmoun
Kreol for an old person or grandparent. Here it means Grandma.
Kuzin
“Cousin” in Mauritian Kreol.
Leak
A crack or hole where water escapes by mistake.
Reservoir
A big lake made to store water for people to use.
Tonton
Uncle.

What would you do?

Kuzin was a drop detective! In Mauritius, more than half of the clean water put into the pipes is lost before it reaches people's taps, mostly through leaks. What would you do?

  • 🚰 You hear a drip-drip in the bathroom at night Turn the tap off tightly, and if it still drips, tell a grown-up so it can be fixed.
  • 🕳️ You see water bubbling out of the road on a dry day Don't touch it! Tell a grown-up, so they can call the CWA on 170 (free) or report it in the KOREK! app.
  • 🪥 Your brother leaves the tap running while he brushes his teeth Show him Kuzin's trick: wet the brush, tap off, brush, then a quick rinse from a cup.
  • 🚿 Your family wants to save water this week Try short showers, catch rainwater for the plants, and check every tap at home. Be a drop detective!

Talk about it with a grown-up or your class. There is more than one good answer!
