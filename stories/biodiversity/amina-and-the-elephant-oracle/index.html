Words to learn

Champions
Heroes who stand up for something and protect it.
Explorers
People who travel to find out about new places.
Greed
Wanting much more of something, like money, than you need.
Oracle
A very wise being in stories, who people visit to ask for advice.
Owl
A bird with big eyes that hunts at night. In many stories owls are wise.
Poachers
People who hunt animals when it is against the law, for example to steal elephants' tusks.
Riddles
Puzzling questions that you have to think hard to answer.
Savannah
Wide, grassy land with a few trees, where it is warm all year.
Tusks
An elephant's two long teeth. Poachers kill elephants to sell them, which is against the law.
Wilderness
Wild land where people don't live.

What would you do?

Amina's village made safe places so the elephants could come home. If you wanted to help elephants and other wild animals, what would you do?

  • 🚫 Never buy anything made from ivory or wild animals Very important! If nobody buys ivory, poachers have no reason to hunt elephants.
  • 🏞️ Help protect the places where wild animals live Yes! Animals need their homes, just like the safe places Amina's village made.
  • 📣 Tell everyone the elephants' story, like Amina did Just like Amina! When people understand, they want to help.
  • 🦉 Learn from wise people who know about nature Like Amina and Kwame the Owl! Rangers and elders know lots about protecting animals.

Talk about it with a grown-up or your class. There is more than one good answer!
