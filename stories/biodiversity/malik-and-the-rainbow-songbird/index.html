Words to learn

Baobab
A giant African tree with a very thick trunk. Some baobabs live for more than a thousand years!
Birdhouses
Little wooden boxes put up in trees, where birds can nest safely.
Charcoal
Black fuel made by slowly burning wood. Making lots of it means cutting down lots of trees.
Deforestation
Cutting down or burning a forest so that it disappears.
Djembe
A West African drum shaped like a goblet, played with bare hands.
Elders
Older, wise people who are respected in the village.
Hummingbird
A tiny bird whose wings beat so fast that they hum.
Tortoise
A slow land animal with a hard shell on its back.
Woodland
Land covered with trees, like a small forest.

What would you do?

Malik's village planted trees and built homes for the birds, and the Rainbow Songbird came back. If the trees near your home were being cut down, what would you do?

  • 🌱 Plant a young tree and look after it Just like Malik's village! Every tree is a home for birds, insects and animals.
  • 🪺 Build a birdhouse with a grown-up Lovely! A birdhouse gives birds a safe place to nest, like the villagers made for the Rainbow Songbird.
  • ♻️ Use less paper and wood, and recycle Smart! Using less means fewer trees need to be cut down.
  • 🥁 Bring people together to protect the forest Like Malik's drum calling the village together! Big changes happen when everyone helps.

Talk about it with a grown-up or your class. There is more than one good answer!
