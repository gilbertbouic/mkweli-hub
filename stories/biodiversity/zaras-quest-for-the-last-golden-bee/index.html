Words to learn

Amber
A warm golden-orange colour.
Baobab
A giant African tree with a very thick trunk. Villages often meet in its shade.
Canopy
The very top of the forest, where the branches and leaves meet like a roof.
Chameleon
A lizard that can change the colour of its skin to match what is around it.
Kaleidoscope
A tube with mirrors and coloured bits inside. When you turn it, you see bright, changing patterns.
Meadows
Fields full of grass and wild flowers.
Mzee
A Swahili word for a respected old man.
Pollen
A fine yellow dust made by flowers. Bees carry it from flower to flower, which helps plants make seeds and fruit.
Pollution
Dirt, smoke, rubbish or chemicals that make the air, water or land unhealthy.
Quest
A long journey to find something important.

What would you do?

Zara saved the flowers by helping the bees. Bees need flowers, and flowers need bees! What could you do to help the bees?

  • 🌼 Plant flowers that bees love Lovely! Bees drink nectar from flowers and carry pollen, so more flowers means more bees.
  • 🚫 Ask grown-ups not to spray poisons on plants Very wise! Some sprays that kill bugs can hurt bees too.
  • 💧 Put out a shallow dish of water with pebbles Clever! Bees get thirsty, and the pebbles give them somewhere safe to land.
  • 🗣️ Tell friends why bees are important Just like Zara! When people know about bees, they want to protect them.

Talk about it with a grown-up or your class. There is more than one good answer!
