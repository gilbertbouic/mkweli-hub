Book 3 · ages 6–10

Rising Sea Levels Across Africa

Three picture stories about children who help protect their seaside homes from the rising sea.

Stories by Gilbert Clement Bouic

▶ Start the first story Buy the book (Barnes & Noble)

In many places along the coast of Africa, the sea is slowly rising. This is dangerous. The sea can wash away land, families may have to leave their homes, and roads, buildings and bridges can be damaged.

Three stories

Stories in this book

1

Lila and the Mermaids Fight Rising Seas

A curious girl sails to an underwater kingdom to help the mermaids calm the rising sea.

15 pages · about 4 min · read-along

2

Amani and Nia Defend Africa's Shores

A brother and sister bring their whole village together to hold back the sea.

10 pages · about 3 min · read-along

3

Ayana and the Rescue of Seashell Bay

Ayana and Granny Zuri use dunes, fences and seagrass to keep the cheeky sea away.

14 pages · about 5 min · read-along
