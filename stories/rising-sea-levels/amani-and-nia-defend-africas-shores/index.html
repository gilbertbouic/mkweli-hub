Words to learn

Ancestors
Family members who lived long, long ago, like your great-great-grandparents.
Baobab
A giant African tree with a very thick trunk. Villages often meet in its shade.
Coast
The land right next to the sea.
Community
All the people who live in a place and look after each other.
Elders
Older, wise people who are respected in the village.
Inventions
New things that nobody has made before.
Mangroves
Trees that grow in salty water along the coast. Their tangled roots hold the sand and slow down big waves.
Tide
The sea coming in and going out again, twice every day.
Traditions
Ways of doing things that families pass on from grandparents to grandchildren.

What would you do?

Kwetu was saved because everyone worked together: children, women, elders and inventors! If the sea was creeping towards your village, what would you do?

  • 🗣️ Tell the elders and the whole village Just like Amani and Nia! When everybody knows about a problem, everybody can help.
  • 🌳 Plant mangroves to protect the coast Brilliant! Mangroves are strong enough to survive wild weather and they slow down the waves.
  • 🧱 Help build a strong wall against big waves Like the villagers of Kwetu! Sea walls help keep homes safe from high seas.
  • 📖 Listen to old stories and share good ideas Very wise! Stories from the past can teach us how to work together today.

Talk about it with a grown-up or your class. There is more than one good answer!
