Words to learn

Coral
Tiny sea animals that build hard, colourful homes together. Lots of coral makes a reef.
Driftwood
Wood that the sea has washed up on the beach.
Explorer
Someone who travels to find out about new places.
Ice caps
Huge sheets of ice near the North and South Poles. When the Earth gets warmer they melt, and the water makes the sea rise.
Mangroves
Trees that grow in salty water along the coast. Their tangled roots hold the sand and slow down big waves.
Mermaid
A magical sea person from stories, with a fish's tail instead of legs.
Mermaids
Magical sea people from stories, with a fish's tail instead of legs.
Ripples
Little rings of waves that spread out across the water.

What would you do?

Lila and her village planted mangroves, and the mermaids helped calm the sea. If the sea was rising near your home, what would you do?

  • 🌳 Plant mangrove trees along the shore Just like Lila's village! Mangrove roots hold the sand and slow down the waves.
  • 🔌 Save energy by switching off lights and screens Great thinking! Using less energy helps stop the Earth from getting warmer, so less ice melts.
  • 🤝 Bring friends and neighbours together to help Just like Lila! Big problems are easier when everyone works together.
  • 🐚 Keep the beach clean and look after sea creatures Wonderful! A healthy sea and beach are better at protecting the coast.

Talk about it with a grown-up or your class. There is more than one good answer!
