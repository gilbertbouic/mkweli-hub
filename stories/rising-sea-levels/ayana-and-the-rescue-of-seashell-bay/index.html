Words to learn

Carbon
Something found in all living things. Too much of it in the air as a gas makes the Earth warmer.
Divers
People who swim under the water, often with special breathing equipment.
Dune grass
Tough grass that grows on sand dunes. Its roots hold the sand in place.
Dunes
Hills of sand made by the wind. On a beach they act like a wall against the sea.
Elder
An older, wise person who is respected in the community.
Floods
When lots of water covers land that is usually dry.
Plains
Large, flat areas of land, often covered in grass.
Sand fences
Fences on the beach that slow the wind, so blown sand piles up into dunes.
Seagrass
Grass-like plants that grow under the sea. They hold the sand and mud on the seabed.

What would you do?

Ayana's village kept the cheeky sea away with dunes, fences and seagrass. If your beach was getting smaller, what would you do?

  • 🌾 Plant dune grasses to hold the sand Just like the children of Seashell Bay! Grass roots keep the sand where it belongs.
  • 🪵 Build sand fences so the dunes grow bigger Clever! Fences slow the wind, and the sand piles up into strong dunes.
  • 🌊 Plant seagrass under the sea Like the village divers! Seagrass holds the seabed and is a home for sea creatures.
  • 🏡 Build new homes further back from the sea Very wise, just like Granny Zuri said! Giving the sea some space keeps homes safe.

Talk about it with a grown-up or your class. There is more than one good answer!
