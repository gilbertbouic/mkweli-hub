Privacy notice

This notice describes how Mkweli handles information when you use our product websites and mobile applications. Last updated: July 2026.

Who we are

Mkweli is a product studio based in Grand Baie, Mauritius. Contact for privacy and support: support@mkweli.tech.

Websites

Our marketing sites (including mkweli.tech and product subdomains) are static pages. We do not run advertising trackers by default. If you email us, we use your address only to respond to your enquiry.

Server or CDN providers (for example GitHub Pages or similar hosts) may process standard technical logs such as IP address, user agent, and request time for security and reliability.

Mobile apps

Several Mkweli apps are designed to work offline or with minimal data:

  • Mkweli AML — sanctions lists can be processed on-device; we do not require cloud accounts for core screening in the free pilot APK.
  • LakazAgri — availability and demand posts you create are for coordination; treat pilot data as non-sensitive and avoid posting personal data you would not share with a buyer or farmer.
  • Assimilate Pro — guide content is local where possible; progress may be stored on your device.
  • CEB Rodrigues Field — field logs stay under the pilot operator’s control; use only for authorised operational testing.

Product-specific privacy details may appear in each app or on its product site. Where they conflict with this page for that product, the product notice takes precedence for that product.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use your pilot enquiries for third-party advertising lists.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@mkweli.tech. Studio or founder enquiries: see About.
